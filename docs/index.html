GOOD TO KNOW

Before you connect.

Do I need a PC or root?

No PC is needed. Install NekoFlash on Android 8.0+ with USB host/OTG support. Root on the host phone is not required; target-side operations depend on its current mode and bootloader state.

What do I need to connect?

Two Android devices and a suitable USB OTG cable or adapter. Authorize USB debugging on the target for ADB, or boot it into the appropriate Fastboot or Recovery mode.

Does it bypass Xiaomi restrictions?

Xiaomi controls unlock eligibility, account binding and waiting periods. NekoFlash does not promise to bypass those conditions. We are collecting real unlock results for the current version.

How do I report a problem?

Include NekoFlash and Android versions, both device models, the action and the outcome. Attach the in-app diagnostic ZIP in Telegram or GitHub Issues. Read the privacy policy for export details.
